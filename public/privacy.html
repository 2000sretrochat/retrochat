YappChat Privacy Policy

This policy explains what YappChat collects when you use the site and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account info: your screen name, a hashed password (we never store it in plain text), and an email address if you give one.
  • IP address and connection logs: YappChat logs the IP address and approximate location (city/region from the IP) of each sign-in and connection. We use this to fight spam, abuse, and ban evasion and to keep the service secure.
  • Profile and settings: avatar, status message, buddy list, block and ignore lists, favorite rooms, and preferences.
  • Messages: room chat is shown live and recent lines are kept in memory for the room. Instant messages to offline buddies are stored until delivered. If you turn on the Message Archive, your IMs are saved to your account until you delete them.
  • Reports: if you report someone, the report (and any message you include) is kept for moderators.

Voice and webcam

Voice and webcam use peer-to-peer WebRTC. Audio and video are not recorded or stored by YappChat. A relay (TURN) server may forward encrypted media when a direct connection is not possible.

How we use it

To run the service, keep it safe, enforce the Terms, and respond to reports. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and local storage

We use a sign-in cookie and your browser's local storage for settings (for example theme, sounds, and audio options). No third-party tracking cookies are set by YappChat itself; if ads are shown, the ad provider may use its own cookies.

Retention and deletion

Connection logs and backups are kept for a limited time. You can delete your message archive at any time in Settings. To ask for account deletion, use the in-app Report button.

Children

YappChat is for adults 18 and over only. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use the service.

Contact

Questions or requests: use the Report button inside YappChat (in a chat room, on a profile, or in an IM window).
